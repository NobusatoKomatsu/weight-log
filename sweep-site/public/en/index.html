How it works

1Everything starts as “keep”

Your installed apps are listed with last-used date and size.

2Untick what you don't need

Unticked apps turn red, and you see how much space you'll free.

3Confirm and remove

Check the full list, then the apps are uninstalled one after another.

What it does

🕒

Find unused apps

Sort by “least recently used” to bring long-forgotten apps to the top.

📦

Find the biggest apps

Sort by size, including each app's data and cache.

🧹

Remove in one go

Selected apps are uninstalled in sequence, and you see the space freed at the end.

Built to prevent mistakes

✅

Everything is kept by default

Only the apps you untick yourself are ever removed.

📋

See it all before it happens

Every app to be removed is listed, and nothing starts until you tick “I have checked”.

☝️

One confirmation per app

Android asks you to confirm each uninstall. You can stop at any time.

🛡️

System apps are excluded

Preinstalled apps never appear in the list, and your unticked choices are not saved.

Privacy

App Sweep reads your list of installed apps, their last-used dates and sizes only to show them on your device. This information is never sent anywhere. Ads (Google AdMob) are shown at the bottom of the screen, but this information is not used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Please note

Uninstalling cannot be undone. Removed apps and their data (sign-in details, saved content and so on) cannot be restored. Back up or transfer anything you need first.
