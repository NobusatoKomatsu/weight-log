Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 ・ 日本語

CHUO KAIKEI K.K. (“we”) sets out below how user information is handled in the Android app “App Sweep for Android” (Japanese name: Androidアプリ一掃アプリ; “the App”). If there is any difference between this English version and the Japanese version, the Japanese version takes precedence.

1. Information the App handles on your device

The App handles the following information only on your device. It is never sent to our servers or to third parties (including advertising providers). We cannot obtain or view it. The App does not store it; it is read from your device each time the screen is opened.

InformationPurpose
List of apps installed on the device (name, icon, install date, whether it came from Google Play)To show a list so you can choose which apps to remove and notice apps you don't remember installing. This covers apps you installed that can be opened from the home screen.
When each app was last usedTo show and sort apps so you can see which ones you no longer use. Read only if you grant “Usage access”.
Storage used by each app (app, data, cache)To show roughly how much space removal will free. Read only if you grant “Usage access” (otherwise only the size of the app itself).

2. Uninstalling apps

The App opens Android's uninstall confirmation only for apps that you have unticked and agreed to remove on the confirmation screen. The actual removal is carried out by Android when you approve it on Android's own confirmation dialog. The App never removes an app without your action.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads at the bottom of the screen using Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads, Google may obtain the following from your device.

InformationPurpose
Advertising ID (an identifier you can reset or delete in device settings)Ad delivery, showing ads based on your interests, frequency capping, fraud prevention.
IP address and the approximate location inferred from itDelivering regionally relevant ads, fraud prevention.
Device information (model, OS version, language, etc.), ad impressions and taps, diagnostic informationAd delivery and measurement, troubleshooting.

This information is sent directly to Google and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. For how Google uses data in advertising, see “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”.

To stop interest-based ads, open your device's Settings, search for “Ads”, and reset or delete your advertising ID from the screen that appears (the menu location varies by device).

The information in section 1 (installed apps, last-used dates, storage) is not used for advertising and is not sent to Google. Apart from advertising, the App does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service.

4. Launch notification (form on the official website)

The “Get notified at launch” form on the official website uses Google Forms, provided by Google LLC. The email address and other information you enter are used only to notify you that the App has been released on Google Play, and are deleted after the notification is sent. They are not provided to third parties. If you no longer want the notification, contact us at the address below and we will delete your information. This form is not part of the App, and the App does not obtain your email address.

5. Permissions used by the App

PermissionPurpose
Usage accessTo show when each app was last used and how much storage it uses. The purpose is explained in the App before you grant it. The App works without it.
Request delete packagesTo open Android's uninstall confirmation for the apps you chose.
Internet access, advertising IDTo show ads (used by the advertising component).

6. Sharing with third parties

We ourselves do not collect user information. The information described in “3. Advertising” is sent to Google for ad delivery. Apart from that, user information is not provided to third parties.

7. Data deletion

The App has no user accounts and we hold no user data. The App does not store user information on the device either. Information obtained by Google for advertising can be reviewed or deleted through your Google Account settings or by deleting your advertising ID as described above. “Usage access” can be revoked at any time in device settings.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised to reflect changes in the law or new features of the App. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
