Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 ・ 日本語

CHUO KAIKEI K.K. (“we”) sets out below how user information is handled in the Android app “App Check for Android” (Japanese name: アプリ整理; “the App”). If there is any difference between this English version and the Japanese version, the Japanese version takes precedence.

1. Information the App handles on your device

The App handles the following information only on your device. It is never sent to our servers or to third parties (including advertising providers). We cannot obtain or view it.

InformationPurpose
List of apps installed on the device (name, icon, added date, source, and the category Android assigns)To show a colour-coded, grouped list so you can notice apps you don't remember installing and apps of the same kind. This covers apps added after purchase that can be opened from the home screen.
When each app was last usedTo colour-code the list so you can see which apps you no longer use. Read only if you grant “Usage access”.
Storage used by each app (app, data, cache)To show each app's size. Read only if you grant “Usage access” (otherwise only the size of the app itself).
Check notes (apps you marked as checked, apps seen in earlier checks, and kinds you changed; all stored as package names)To flag apps added since last time and to distinguish checked apps the next time you open the App. Stored only on your device, and removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in device settings.

2. Uninstalling apps

The App opens Android's uninstall confirmation only for an app you chose to uninstall on its details screen. The actual removal is carried out by Android when you approve it on Android's own confirmation dialog. The App never removes an app without your action.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads at the bottom of the screen using Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads, Google may obtain the following from your device.

InformationPurpose
Advertising ID (an identifier you can reset or delete in device settings)Ad delivery, showing ads based on your interests, frequency capping, fraud prevention.
IP address and the approximate location inferred from itDelivering regionally relevant ads, fraud prevention.
Device information (model, OS version, language, etc.), ad impressions and taps, diagnostic informationAd delivery and measurement, troubleshooting.

This information is sent directly to Google and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. For how Google uses data in advertising, see “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”.

To stop interest-based ads, open your device's Settings, search for “Ads”, and reset or delete your advertising ID from the screen that appears (the menu location varies by device).

The information in section 1 (installed apps, last-used dates, storage) is not used for advertising and is not sent to Google. Apart from advertising, the App does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service.

4. The official website

The “Get notified at launch” form on the official website (application.blue) uses Google Forms, provided by Google LLC. The email address you enter is used only to notify you that the App has been released on Google Play, and is deleted after the notification is sent. It is not provided to third parties. If you no longer want the notification, contact us at the address below and we will delete it. This form is not part of the App, and the App does not obtain your email address.

5. Permissions used by the App

PermissionPurpose
Usage accessTo show when each app was last used and how much storage it uses. The purpose is explained in the App before you grant it. The App works without it.
Request delete packagesTo open Android's uninstall confirmation for an app you chose to uninstall.
Internet access, advertising IDTo show ads (used by the advertising component).

6. Sharing with third parties

We ourselves do not collect user information. The information described in “3. Advertising” is sent to Google for ad delivery. Apart from that, user information is not provided to third parties.

7. Data deletion

The App has no user accounts and we hold no user data. The check notes stored on your device (section 1) are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in device settings. Information obtained by Google for advertising can be reviewed or deleted through your Google Account settings or by deleting your advertising ID as described above. “Usage access” can be revoked at any time in device settings.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised to reflect changes in the law or new features of the App. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
