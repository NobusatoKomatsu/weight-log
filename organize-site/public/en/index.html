Colours are decided only from facts: when the app was added, when it was last used, and where it came from. They do not judge whether an app is safe (this is not a virus scanner). The reason for each colour is shown for every app.

Look at the details, then mark the app as checked or uninstall it.

What it does

Notice new arrivals

Each time you open it, apps added since your last check are flagged, so nothing slips in unnoticed.

Details for every app

Added date, last used, storage breakdown and source. You can also open the Play Store page or Android settings.

See overlaps

Apps are grouped into 13 kinds such as games, maps and travel, and money. Kinds are guessed from names and can be changed by you.

Uninstall one at a time

Remove an app from its details screen. Android always asks you to confirm.

Please note

Because of how Android works, this app cannot rearrange your home screen icons. Use the suggested layout as a guide and move them yourself. Uninstalled apps and their data cannot be restored.

Privacy

App Check reads your list of installed apps, their last-used dates and sizes only to show them on your device. This information is never sent anywhere. Ads (Google AdMob) are shown at the bottom of the screen, but this information is not used for advertising. See the Privacy Policy for details.
